💪 BODYWEIGHT PROTOCOL NOTES
  • Zero equipment. Zero excuses. Every set is RPE 9–10 — if it feels manageable, go faster or add reps.
  • No rest between exercises within a superset. Only rest after the full round (and keep it short).
  • Pistol squats: hold a doorframe, tree, or bench edge for balance assist — lower as deep as possible.
  • Failure sets mean true failure — when you hit it, do 3 rest-pause reps with a 5s break to squeeze more out.
  • The Coffin finisher is done once, not twice. Go at absolute maximum from the first rep.
  • 30 minutes, all bodyweight, nowhere to hide — this should feel as hard as the 60-minute session.

💪 ARMS PROTOCOL NOTES
  • Run this as a dedicated arm day, or stack it after the AM session if you want maximum volume in a single day.
  • The 21s curl protocol is non-negotiable — partial reps in each range force constant tension across the full ROM.
  • Chin-up iso holds: the :10 hold at 90° + 5s negative is more effective than 5 standard chin-ups for bicep hypertrophy.
  • Anchor your band to any bar or post for pushdowns and overhead extensions — the angle changes the stimulus significantly.
  • Forearm work is not optional. Grip strength is your limiting factor on every pull movement. Build it.
  • Failure sets: when you hit true failure, rest 5 seconds, then squeeze out 3 more. That's where growth happens.
  • Expect significant soreness in the brachialis and long head tricep if you hit the iso holds and overhead work properly.

🏋️ GYM PROTOCOL NOTES
  • Superset rule applies here too — no rest between exercises within a block, only rest after the full round.
  • Weighted pull-ups: grip a dumbbell between crossed feet, or loop a belt around one. Start lighter than you think.
  • Cable exercises: reset weight between sets only if needed — minimize downtime, keep heart rate elevated.
  • Dumbbell Bulgarian split squats are the hardest leg exercise in this session. Don't skip reps — each one counts double.
  • Romanian deadlifts: the stretch is the stimulus. If you're not feeling it in the hamstrings at the bottom, your hinge is wrong.
  • Failure sets at the end: rest-pause allowed — hit failure, rest 5s, squeeze out 3 more. That's where gains live.
  • Eat a full meal 60–90 minutes before this session. 90 minutes of this volume on an empty stomach is not the move.
